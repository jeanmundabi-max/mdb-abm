Why it happens

Why a card fails and a bank mandate does not

A card needs the money to be there on the day

If the balance is short by a pound, the payment is declined. The member finds out afterwards, usually from a fee.

Your member said this themselves

They wrote that with a Direct Debit they could top the bank up before it went out. With a card they had no warning and no chance.

A mandate pulls on a schedule the payer can see

Direct Debit gives advance notice by design. The payer knows the date and the amount before the money moves.
