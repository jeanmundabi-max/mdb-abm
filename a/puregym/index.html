The five checks

What I would look at,
in this order.

01

What share of collections sit on a card rather than a bank mandate

Card is the rail that fails when a balance is short. A mandate is not. If you do not know the split, you do not know your exposure.

02

What the first failure costs the member

Two reviewers name it: a reconnection fee and an admin fee. Both are charged for a mechanism the member did not choose.

03

Whether the member can see it coming

The reviewer's own point. With a mandate they can top up before it goes out. With a card they find out afterwards.

04

Whether the fee recovers more than it costs

A recovered fee and a one-star review naming the amount are not the same size. Nobody usually measures the second.

05

What the review feed says after a rail change

The words reconnection fee should stop appearing. That is a measurable outcome, not a promise.
