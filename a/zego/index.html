The five checks

What I would look at,
in this order.

01

What the inactivity fee actually recovers

Nine reviewers name an amount. Set against the policies cancelled and the reviews written, the net is worth calculating.

02

Whether auto top-up can fire more than once

A reviewer says it took multiple payments at once. Either it can or it cannot, and the answer should not come from a review.

03

Whether a charge is dated when it happened

One reviewer reports a payment deducted today showing under tomorrow. A rider reconciling against a bank statement cannot match that.

04

What a rider sees before money moves

Prepaid credit moves silently. A mandate gives advance notice by design.

05

Whether prepaid has to be the only option

Riders earn irregularly. A rail that collects when work happened is a different product to one that holds a balance.
