Why it happens

Why prepaid credit creates the fees riders complain about

Prepaid needs a balance sitting there

So it runs out, and it runs out fastest for the riders working least. Your reviewers describe topping up every few days.

Fees for inactivity are a consequence of the model

A rider who does not work still holds a policy, so the account still costs something. They experience that as being charged for nothing.

A mandate collects when something happened

No stored balance, no top-up, nothing to expire. The charge maps to the work.
