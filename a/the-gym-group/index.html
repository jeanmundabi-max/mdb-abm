Objections

The questions worth asking

We are already mid programme on member management and payments. Why now?

Because the two things are not the same. Your own annual report separates them: the systems deliver referrals, retention strategies and improved payment failure rates. The first two are software. The third is a rail. The programme completes in 2026 and interim results are 9 September, so a baseline that separates a bounce from a cancellation is worth having before then, not after.

What if the failure rate does not move?

Then you will know, which is more than the current data can tell you. Your accounts carry a £1.0m receivable and a nil loss allowance, so a failed collection never appears as a balance. It appears as churn in a different report. Measuring it is the first step, not the last, and nobody outside The Gym Group can tell you what your rate is today.

We already retry failed payments.

So did the Norwegian Refugee Council, who described it as basically chucking attempts at the wall. Their published result was a 7% increase in recapture compared to just retrying. The difference is not whether you retry, it is which day.

Is this not just Direct Debit, which we already use?

Partly. Direct Debit is how the money moves. Success+ is what happens when a collection on that rail fails, and Verified Mandates is what stops a bad account number becoming a failure in month one. If you already collect by Direct Debit, the question is what your retry logic does on day two.

Our members complain about cancelling, not about payments.

Yes, and we are not going to pretend otherwise. Forty of your five hundred most recent reviews mention cancelling, thirty three of them at one or two stars. It matters here because a member who cannot leave cleanly sometimes stops the money instead, and that arrives in your data looking like a failure.

What does GoCardless actually claim?

That Success+ recovers up to 70% of failed payments. Up to is their word and we have kept it. Separately, they report that 70% of businesses said Success+ improves customer relationships. Those are two different figures that happen to share a number, and they should not be merged.
