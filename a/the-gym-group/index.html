The five checks

What I would look at,
in this order.

01

When a cancellation actually reaches you

The day the member decides, or the day a collection fails. Those are different dates and only one of them is honest.

02

What the member pays to leave mid-month

A reviewer calls it paying extra for cancellation. Whatever the intent, that is how it reads to the person writing the review.

03

Where the cancel-at-the-bank route went

A member describes it as the old, straightforward way. Removing it moved the work to your support queue.

04

How many failed collections are really cancellations

If leaving is hard, some members stop the money instead. Those look like failures in your data and like leavers in reality.

05

What a mandate hands back

The member can stop it at their bank, you see it the same day, and the last thing they do is not write a review.
