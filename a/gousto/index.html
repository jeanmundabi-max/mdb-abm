The five checks

What I would look at,
in this order.

01

Whether a pre-authorisation is needed at all

It is a card mechanism. It holds the customer's money before anything is owed, and your reviewer noticed to the pound.

02

How long a hold sits on their balance

The reviewer says hopefully it will time out. That sentence is the whole problem.

03

Whether a pause reaches billing before the charge does

One reviewer paused, got the confirmation email, and was charged anyway. Two systems, one of them slower.

04

What happens between cancelled and the next box

A reviewer says boxes and charges both continued for two weeks.

05

What collecting on the day it is owed changes

No hold, no pre-auth, no money sitting where neither of you can use it.
