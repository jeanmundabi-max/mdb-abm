Why it happens

Why a pre-authorisation costs you goodwill

A pre-auth is a card mechanism

It ring-fences the customer's money before anything is owed. Your reviewer watched 38 pounds sit there and wrote hopefully it will time out.

A pause has to reach billing before the charge does

One reviewer paused, received the confirmation, and was charged anyway. Two systems, one slower than the other.

A mandate has no hold

There is nothing to ring-fence. The collection happens on the day, for the amount, and not before.
