A renewal is the only moment the price moves

A framework sets what a supplier may charge at most. The call-off sets what you actually pay, and the call-off is the thing being renewed. For the term after that, the number is settled and no amount of good management moves it.

That is the whole reason this page has a date on it rather than a season.
